Student Navigator

7 steps for first-year students

  1. 01

    Get to know your advisor and curator

    Your first helpers and guides at the university are your advisor and curator.

    They:

    • explain how the academic process works
    • help you choose your courses
    • support you as you settle into university life

    Tip: if a question comes up, don't put it off — reach out right away.

  2. 02

    Use Platonus every day!

    The university's main digital platform is your everyday assistant.

    Here you'll find:

    • your class schedule
    • course syllabi
    • assignments
    • grades
    • learning materials

    A five-minute check-in saves you from last-minute rushing and surprises.

  3. 03

    The library — a space for focused study!

    The university library isn't just a place to borrow books.

    It has:

    • reading rooms
    • academic literature
    • digital resources
    • a comfortable space for independent study

    Good results often start with a well-organized space.

  4. 04

    Be an active student!

    Amanzholov University runs a Youth Affairs Committee and dozens of student organizations.

    Pick a direction that speaks to you:

    • SHABYT — student theatre
    • DILMAR — debate club
    • MEYIRIM and SHUAQ — volunteer organizations
    • THE GREEN WAVE
    • Mind Matters Club
    • Oqyrman and others

    Joining just one organization can open up dozens of new opportunities.

  5. 05

    Don't be afraid to ask for help!

    You don't have to solve every problem on your own.

    The university has:

    • a psychological support service
    • a student affairs and social issues office
    • advisors and curators

    Seeing a psychologist is:

    • confidential
    • safe
    • by appointment

    Asking for help isn't weakness — it's the right call.

  6. 06

    Your safety is in your hands!

    Three rules every student should remember:

    • don't share your personal data with strangers
    • never give out your banking codes
    • don't trust suspicious messages or dubious job offers

    Also follow the university's internal rules and safety requirements.

  7. 07

    Think of the university as a space full of possibilities!

    Amanzholov University gives students opportunities for:

    • academic mobility
    • joining research clubs
    • running student projects
    • career development

    What you do today is the foundation of what you'll achieve tomorrow.

Remember!

The university isn't just where you get a diploma. It's a place where you find yourself, make friends, gain experience, and shape your future.
